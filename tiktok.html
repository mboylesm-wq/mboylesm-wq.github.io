♞ Connect TikTok

PnoyChess Poster - one-time authorization for the PnoyChess TikTok account.

PnoyChess Poster is the channel owner's own publishing tool. Connecting lets it:

  • user.info.basic - confirm which TikTok account was connected;
  • video.upload - send PnoyChess's own videos to that account as drafts, which the owner reviews and posts in the TikTok app.

No data about other TikTok users is read or stored. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services. See the privacy policy and terms of service.
