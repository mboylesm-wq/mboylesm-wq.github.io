♞ PnoyChess

Chess stories, classic games and puzzles from the Philippines and the world.

PnoyChess is a Filipino chess channel on Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok. It publishes short chess reels (classic games with commentary from public-domain sources, and daily puzzles) and long-form chess documentaries.

PnoyChess Poster

PnoyChess Poster is the channel owner's own publishing tool. It is used only by PnoyChess to:

  • upload PnoyChess's own videos to PnoyChess's own Facebook Page, Instagram, YouTube channel and TikTok account;
  • set titles, descriptions, captions, thumbnails and publishing times for those videos;
  • read the channel's own statistics to improve future videos.

It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's accounts. See the privacy policy and terms of service.

YouTube

PnoyChess Poster uses YouTube API Services to upload and manage videos on the PnoyChess YouTube channel (@pnoychessofficialyt). By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
